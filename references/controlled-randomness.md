# Controlled Randomness

Use this only when the user requests random, surprise, multiple, or exploratory variants.

## Invariants

Keep these stable across a set:

- the cover-derived field, shadow, neutral and semantic signal roles;
- the album's value structure and emotional temperature;
- one abstract album symbol;
- real-species morphology, mature scale and arrangement feasibility;
- the quality gate and material-credibility standard;
- no literal cover content, face, title, logo or legible text inside the generated floral scene; add only the exact source cover as the final deterministic comparison card.

## Variables

Vary at least three of these between outputs:

- structural mode;
- size tier;
- hero-flower family;
- core density;
- backboard geometry;
- cradle material;
- one optional transformed material;
- directional gesture;
- camera treatment and setting.

Do not generate three outputs that differ only by flower species or saturation.

Each selected variant produces one independent `Gift Bouquet` by default. If the user asks for three variants, deliver three bouquets—not six paired scenes. `Home Living Art` is generated only when explicitly requested; when requested alongside a bouquet, vary its support system, spatial composition and camera treatment rather than reusing one render on a different background.

## Compatible colour profiles

- `soft-mineral`: chalk, shell, grey-pink, mist lilac, sage; soft petals and fibrous paper.
- `electric-botanical`: teal, cobalt, violet, silver, black; one controlled pigment or metallic family.
- `dark-couture`: black, graphite, aubergine, bronze; hierarchy through reflectance, not flat black recolouring.
- `natural-signal`: ivory, plant green, raw fibre and one small yellow, orange, red or blue signal.

## Useful generation count

Default to two variants for selection. Use three when the user explicitly wants exploration. More than three usually reduces review quality and should be a later batch.

## Reproducible sampling

When the skill is local, run:

```bash
python3 scripts/randomize_prescription.py --profile electric-botanical --count 3 --seed "album-title"
```

The script proposes compatible structural variables. Replace its generic palette language with visible evidence from the actual cover before generating.
